ABOUT JACK

HI, I'M JACK.

I’ve been the outsider trying to figure out how things work many times. Five years at an international school in Yokohama, where I picked up Japanese, and now I’m in Milan for a semester. It taught me that if something only makes sense to me, it’s not done yet. So I do the research, make every choice on purpose, and test with real people before I call anything finished.

I started with Cool Math Games, making logos on whatever free site let me export, and building Wix websites for school projects even when nobody asked. Now, I’m doing user research, brand systems, and prototyping. Same instincts, just with better tools.

Away from the screen, you’ll find me on a hiking trail, basketball court, or playing a card game with my friends.

Faith is also a big part of who I am. Easter at Riverside is one of my favorite projects, mostly because of the team: kind, incredibly knowledgeable people who let me soak up everything they knew about their field when they absolutely didn’t have to.
