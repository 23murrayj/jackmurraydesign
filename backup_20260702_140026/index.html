ABOUT JACK

HI, I'M JACK.

I'm a UX designer studying at Bradley University, but my work reaches into a lot of different places: websites, brand systems, graphics, photography, motion, coding, and automation. I like being able to move between ideas and figure out what a project actually needs, whether that is a cleaner interface, a stronger visual identity, or a better way for people to move through something.

I grew up making logos, using computers, and getting pulled into the internet through games, websites, and creative tools. That mix still shapes how I design now. I care about work that feels intentional, useful, and human — design that looks strong, but also makes sense.

My faith is also part of who I am, and some of my favorite work has come from church graphics and creative projects that connect design with meaning. I'm still learning, still experimenting, and still building a portfolio that reflects the full range of what I'm interested in.
